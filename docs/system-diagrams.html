Table 1

Data Stores and Their Database Tables

Each data store in Figures 2 and 3 maps to these tables in the Supabase PostgreSQL database.

Data storeTablesHolds
D1 Usersprofiles (+ Supabase Auth users)Name, role (resident, business, admin), active status
D2 Businessesbusinesses, business_documents, business_hours, business_materialsShop profile, DTI/SEC, permit and BIR documents, hours, accepted materials and prices
D3 Listingslistings, listing_images, categoriesProducts, stock, status (draft, pending, active, rejected), photos
D4 Cartcart_itemsItems a resident intends to buy
D5 Ordersorders, order_itemsOrders per shop, pickup schedule, status, stock-held flag
D6 Paymentspayments, refunds, webhook_eventsPayMongo payment IDs, refund records, processed webhook events
D7 Drop-offsdrop_offs, drop_off_itemsRecyclables brought to shops, weights, estimated value
D8 Forumforum_threads, forum_posts, forum_reactions, forum_thread_reactions, forum_bookmarksCommunity posts, replies, reactions, saved posts
D9 Messagesconversations, messages, conversation_settingsDirect messages, mute, archive, block
D10 NotificationsnotificationsIn-app notifications and read status
D11 ReviewsreviewsShop and product ratings, shop replies
D12 Reports and Auditreports, admin_audit_log, system_settingsUser and content reports, admin actions, platform settings

Uploaded files (product photos, business documents, logos, avatars) are kept in Supabase Storage buckets, and their links are saved in the tables above.
